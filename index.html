PaoliFest: Echoes

Let Music Speak’s flagship program, PaoliFest, was a free music and arts festival that brought nationally and internationally touring artists to the small town of Paoli, Indiana, to perform alongside local musicians and young artists. From 2018 to 2023, the festival welcomed thousands of attendees and featured artists from across the country. PaoliFest: Echoes carries that spirit forward through concerts, visiting artists, creative collaborations, and community outreach throughout Southern Indiana.

The Concert Comes to You

An outreach concert program for places whose residents may have limited transportation or difficulty attending traditional venues. We bring thoughtfully programmed live performances directly to retirement and senior living communities, care facilities, and other residential settings, with ensembles selected to suit each audience and space.

Bring Live Music to You

Partnerships & Collaborations

We collaborate with partners such as Team Peace and the Lotus Education & Arts Foundation to create workshops, artist residencies, and original performances shaped by the people and places they serve.

Recent Projects

PaoliFest: Echoes · 2026

A Night in Argentina

Continuo joined visiting artists Eleanor Dubinsky and Dario Acosta Teich for an evening of Argentine music at Blueberry Hill Estate. The residency also brought performances and outreach to four community locations, reaching approximately 200 adults and children.

Interactive Concert · 2025

String Quartet Jukebox

At the Lost River Co-op & Cafe, audience members received the evening’s musical menu and chose what Continuo played next—from classical works to familiar songs arranged for string quartet.

Youth Workshop

Harmony & Heart

This interactive workshop uses live music to explore friendship, teamwork, and the difference between dissonance and harmony—giving young people a shared language for listening, collaboration, and connection.

PaoliFest: Echoes · 2024

Continuo with Dave Howard & Chris “Roadie” Rodahaffer

PaoliFest: Echoes began with an intimate concert at Blueberry Hill Estate featuring Continuo alongside Southern Indiana musicians Dave Howard and Chris “Roadie” Rodahaffer—a new way to continue PaoliFest’s artistic and community work.

About Let Music Speak

Let Music Speak is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit arts organization directed by professional violinist Jordana Greenberg.

Our work began in Paoli, Indiana, and has grown through concerts, collaborations, workshops, and community programs in Indiana, Ohio, and beyond.
