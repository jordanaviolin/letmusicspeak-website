About Let Music Speak

Why We Exist

Our Mission

We believe that music and art programming can be transformative for families, individuals, and the places they call home, and that everyone should have access to these experiences.

We partner with local organizations and residents to present festivals, performances, workshops, and creative events. Our programs bring extraordinary live music to small towns, schools, retirement centers, and other gathering spaces where access may be limited.

Let Music Speak’s flagship program, PaoliFest, was a free music and arts festival that brought nationally and internationally touring artists to the small town of Paoli, Indiana, to perform alongside local musicians and young artists. From 2018 to 2023, the festival welcomed thousands of attendees and featured artists from across the country. PaoliFest: Echoes carries that spirit forward through concerts, visiting artists, creative collaborations, and community outreach throughout Southern Indiana.

Leadership

Jordana Greenberg, Director

Jordana Greenberg is a professional violinist, singer, and songwriter and the director of Let Music Speak. Originally from Ontario, Canada, Jordana was raised in Paoli, Indiana, and is now a resident of Cincinnati, Ohio. She leads the organization’s artistic programming and partnerships, including PaoliFest and PaoliFest: Echoes.
