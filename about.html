About Let Music Speak

Let Music Speak

Let Music Speak is a nonprofit arts organization that brings extraordinary live music to people and places with limited access to it, including small towns, schools, retirement centers, and other gathering spaces.

We create memorable concerts, workshops, and collaborative projects shaped around the people and places they serve.

Let Music Speak’s flagship program, PaoliFest, was a free music and arts festival that brought nationally and internationally touring artists to the small town of Paoli, Indiana, to perform alongside local musicians and young artists. From 2018 to 2023, the festival welcomed thousands of attendees and featured artists from across the country. PaoliFest: Echoes carries that spirit forward through concerts, visiting artists, creative collaborations, and outreach throughout Southern Indiana.

Our Mission

We believe music and arts programming can be transformative for communities, families, and individuals, and that everyone should have access to these experiences. We partner with community members and organizations to present festivals, performances, workshops, and creative events.

Let Music Speak is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

Leadership

Jordana Greenberg, Director

Jordana Greenberg is a professional violinist, singer, and songwriter and the director of Let Music Speak. Raised in Paoli, Indiana, she leads the organization’s artistic programming and partnerships, including PaoliFest and PaoliFest: Echoes.
