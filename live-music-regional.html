The Concert Comes to You

Professional live music, right where residents live.

Let Music Speak brings extraordinary live music to the places people live, learn, gather, and connect.

Our engaging, thoughtfully programmed concerts bring accomplished professional musicians directly into senior-living communities and residential care settings.

Ask About a Performance
Designed for your community

A concert that feels personal.

Programs combine beautiful live performance with friendly introductions, stories about the music, and opportunities for residents to listen, remember, and connect.

We shape each program around your residents, room, season, and occasion. Performances typically run 45–60 minutes.

Let Music Speak handles the musicians, repertoire, contracting, scheduling, and performance coordination.

Choose your ensemble

Two distinctive ways to experience live strings.

Seven Hills String Duo

Violin • Guitar

Violin and guitar create a warm, amplified sound with improvisation, jazz standards, Beatles favorites, rock and classic rock, Broadway and film songs, and requests from across the decades.

The duo brings its own compact sound tower and all required performance equipment. The community provides two armless chairs and access to one electrical outlet.

Visit Seven Hills String Duo

Let Music Speak Quartet

Two violins • Viola • Cello

Four professional string players bring the depth, resonance, and elegance of a full acoustic chamber ensemble, performing classical masterworks alongside arrangements of contemporary pop and Broadway favorites.

Let Music Speak selects accomplished freelance musicians from the region and coordinates the complete performance. The community provides four armless chairs; no amplification or special equipment is required.

Professional musicians, thoughtfully matched.

Exceptional music can happen anywhere.

Let Music Speak works with trusted professional musicians in each region to create polished, welcoming concerts close to home.

Every program is selected for artistic quality, audience connection, and an inviting performance style.

For outdoor performances, the full performance area must be covered and protected from direct sun and rain.

Why Let Music Speak?

Because we’re musicians too.

Let Music Speak is directed by professional violinist Jordana Greenberg, who has spent her career performing and working alongside professional musicians throughout the country.

We know wonderful players, and we believe there are audiences everywhere who would love the chance to hear them.

Our job is simply to bring the two together.

Let’s bring live music to your community.

Tell us about your residents, preferred date, performance space, and musical interests. We’ll recommend the ensemble and shape the program with you.
