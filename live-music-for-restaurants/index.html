Our featured restaurant ensemble · Violin and guitar

Queen City Duo

An inviting sound for dinner service, cocktail hours, and intimate spaces. Queen City Duo pairs expressive violin with the warmth and rhythm of guitar, moving naturally between refined and relaxed.

  • Jazz, classical, folk, and contemporary music
  • Improvisation and flexible programming
  • Compact footprint for intimate or active spaces

Especially suited to: dinner service, cocktail hours, brunches, patios, and intimate dining rooms where music should enhance conversation.

For special events · String quartet

Aster String Quartet

A quartet of accomplished Cincinnati musicians brings depth, polish, and the unmistakable presence of live strings.

  • Classical masterworks
  • Contemporary pop and Broadway arrangements
  • Background music or featured performance

Especially suited to: wine dinners, holiday evenings, milestone celebrations, and private events that call for the rich sound of four string players.

Simple from the start

Start with one evening.

1

Share the basics.
Tell us about your restaurant, preferred date, service hours, available space, and budget. A single evening is a good place to begin; recurring dates can be discussed too.

2

We recommend the right fit.
We’ll discuss Queen City Duo’s repertoire, volume, setup, and breaks around your service—or explore Aster String Quartet for a special event. You’ll receive a tailored quote before committing.

3

We handle the music.
Let Music Speak coordinates the musicians, contract, schedule, and invoice, giving your team one point of contact.
