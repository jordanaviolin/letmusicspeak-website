Live Music for Your Community

Professional musicians. Great music. Right where you are.

Let Music Speak brings professional live music directly to retirement and senior living communities.

Our string quartets perform a mix of iconic classical music and familiar rock, pop, jazz, and show tunes—all in the rich, unmistakable sound of live strings.

Ask About a Performance

The String Quartet

Two violins • Viola • Cello

Let Music Speak works with accomplished professional musicians to bring live string quartet performances directly into senior communities.

Programs range from beloved classical works to music residents know from across the decades: rock and pop favorites, jazz standards, Broadway and show tunes, and more.

Performances typically run 45–60 minutes.

We take care of the musicians, music, contracting, and logistics. You provide the audience.

Continuo String Quartet

Two violins • Viola • Cello

Continuo is a Cincinnati chamber music collective known for performances that move easily between musical worlds.

For Let Music Speak performances, the quartet draws from classical favorites alongside pop, rock, film music, and recognizable songs arranged for strings.

Seven Hills String Duo

Violin • Guitar

Seven Hills String Duo pairs the lyricism of violin with the warmth and rhythm of guitar.

The duo’s extensive repertoire of familiar music spanning decades and genres makes it a natural fit for intimate resident concerts, happy hours, celebrations, and special events.

Why Let Music Speak?

Because we’re musicians too.

Let Music Speak is directed by professional violinist Jordana Greenberg, who has spent her career performing and working alongside professional musicians throughout the country.

We know wonderful players, and we believe there are audiences everywhere who would love the chance to hear them.

Our job is simply to bring the two together.

Let’s bring live music to your community.

Tell us a little about your residents, your space, and what you’re looking for. We’ll take it from there.
