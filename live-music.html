Live Music for Residential Settings

The Concert Comes to You

Thoughtfully programmed live performances for retirement and senior living communities, care facilities, and other residential settings.

Designed for residents who may have limited transportation or difficulty attending traditional venues, each concert brings the immediacy and beauty of live music directly into a familiar space.

Ask About a Concert
The Program

A concert shaped for your residents.

Programs may feature a string quartet, violin-and-guitar duo, jazz trio, wind ensemble, vocal group, or another configuration suited to your audience and space.

Repertoire can move from beloved classical works to jazz standards, Broadway, film music, and familiar songs from across the decades.

Concerts typically run 45–60 minutes. We coordinate the musicians, repertoire, contracting, and performance logistics with your staff.

What to Expect

Simple for your staff. Meaningful for your residents.

Professional artists

Accomplished musicians selected for both artistic quality and an inviting performance style.

Flexible programming

Music and ensemble size chosen around your residents, room, occasion, and budget.

Handled by LMS

We coordinate personnel, repertoire, contracting, and performance details from one point of contact.

Planning

What we’ll ask you.

Tell us about your residents, the performance space, the date or occasion, and any musical interests you would like us to consider.

We’ll recommend an ensemble and program that fit. Pricing varies by group size, location, and scheduling; every proposal includes a clear fee before you book.
