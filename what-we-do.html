What We Do

Let Music Speak brings extraordinary live music to the places people live, learn, gather, and connect.

PaoliFest & PaoliFest: Echoes

Let Music Speak’s flagship program, PaoliFest, was a free music and arts festival that brought nationally and internationally touring artists to the small town of Paoli, Indiana, to perform alongside local musicians and young artists. From 2018 to 2023, the festival welcomed thousands of attendees and featured artists from across the country.

PaoliFest: Echoes carries that spirit forward through concerts, visiting artists, creative collaborations, and community outreach throughout Southern Indiana.

The Concert Comes to You

An outreach concert program for places whose residents may have limited transportation or difficulty attending traditional venues.

We bring thoughtfully programmed live performances directly to retirement and senior living communities, care facilities, and other residential settings. Programs may feature string quartets, violin-and-guitar duos, jazz trios, wind ensembles, vocal groups, and more.

Creative Partnerships

Let Music Speak works with local and regional organizations to create programs that combine live performance with their expertise and relationships.

With Team Peace, we develop school workshops based on its mindfulness and resilience-building curriculum. Music & Emotions combined live performance, interactive music-making, and conversation to help students explore how music can express emotion and influence their reactions.

Through the Lotus Education & Arts Foundation, we partnered with Argentine artists Eleanor Dubinsky and Dario Acosta Teich and Continuo to create A Night in Argentina, an original collaborative performance and residency that reached audiences at four Southern Indiana locations.

Past Projects

Margaret Hamilton Mural

In 2019, Let Music Speak commissioned a public artwork honoring Paoli native Margaret Hamilton, the pioneering computer scientist and lead software engineer for NASA’s Apollo program.

The Heartland Hustle

Created in partnership with So In Body, the Heartland Hustle combined movement and public art through a 5K run/walk along a route featuring artwork created by local youth, adults, and professional artists.
